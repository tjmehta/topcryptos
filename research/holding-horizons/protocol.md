# Holding horizons relative to the viewed interval

Frozen before this new calculation, 2026-09-13. Existing studies and outcomes have already been examined: this is an exploratory extension, not an untouched holdout or a search for a deployable winner. No production changes or new data acquisition.

Question: for a ranking formed over the selected chart window, how do subsequent returns change with holding time? Do short-window signals justify long holds? Distinguish identifying a future high from executing an exit.

## Fixed comparison

- Reuse the hash-verified 2020–2025 annual Binance cohorts and identity-separated panel from `../algorithm-comparison/high-flier-data/final`. Preserve membership through exits, missing prices, and identity breaks. Never join into a different annual cohort to recover an unavailable future price.
- Daily view settings: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 14, 21, 30, 45, 60, 90 observations. The app subtracts `amount - 1`: momentum uses close[t] / close[t-(amount-1)]. This is a price-signal comparison matching the observation count, not a recreation of Classic, live API responses, personal filters, or calendar-month signals.
- Signal-only eligibility: reuse the frozen loader and eligibility (60 complete consecutive daily bars; previous-20-day median quote volume >= 1 million USDT), plus a complete consecutive viewed window. The source annual roster was selected using preceding-December availability and turnover. This omits within-year new listings; the sample is not global top-500 coverage.
- Two pre-existing candidate families: positive endpoint momentum; positive OLS slope of log close multiplied by R-squared, with positive endpoint return required. Rank within the exact same eligible universe; stable identity-key ties; at most 10 positions, equal 10% slots, unfilled slots cash. Full signal-time eligible universe is an equal-weight benchmark, including negative-momentum coins. Report it separately because exposure differs when slots are empty.
- Signal dates: January 1 plus 28*k calendar days, k=0..11, in each cohort year. Holdings: 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days. The same dates and selected IDs are used across those holdings. For 365-day holdings use only k=0..2 because the frozen source ends the following February. Also report every shorter holding on that same first-three-date subset. This is an administratively limited, seasonally concentrated annual comparison; it does not establish a general one-year holding policy. All viewed windows share this calendar, although insufficient formation history may reduce early-year coverage for the 90-observation view.
- Entry: daily open t+2 after signal close t. Fixed exit: daily open entry+H. Reuse executable-open checks (midnight alignment, positive price and trading activity). Do not replace unavailable selected coins. Missing entry is cash; missing exit after entry is retained under gross 0% and -100% scenarios, with 50bps fees each side using the existing exact unit-capital formula. Missing-price marks are sensitivity conventions, not realized losses or estimates.
- Per selection record endpoint net returns, entry/exit missing flags, known sampled daily-high +20% hits, complete-path maximum favorable excursion and downside. All future highs are evaluation labels only. A known hit may survive an incomplete path; no-hit on an incomplete path remains unknown. No fills at the high, no annualized returns, no compounded strategy wealth. Holdings overlap for H>28; neither positions nor overlapping dates are independent observations.
- Summarize each method/view/holding by year, all years, 2020–2022, 2023–2025, and common first-three-date subset. Prior results informed the research; later years are retrospective diagnostics, not a holdout. Report arithmetic mean per cohort and coin-level median conditional on known exits. Cost scenarios are unit trades, not a capacity/slippage/portfolio simulation.
- No threshold tuning, model fitting, best-window selector, claims of calibrated probabilities or future rank, confidence intervals, or inferential winner claims. Existing fixed-versus-triggered exit results are discussed separately, not silently extrapolated to new holds.
