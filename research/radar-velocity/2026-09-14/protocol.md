# Radar boundary and velocity replay

Frozen before new results on September 14, 2026 UTC. This is a new retrospective hypothesis test on previously examined data, not an untouched holdout or a forecast calibration study. No production scorer changes are authorized by a favorable cell alone.

Source: the frozen September 13 CMC inputs, all 71 daily observations and six separate hourly blocks. Primary view: seven daily observations. Sensitivities: daily 3/14/30 and hourly 3/6/12. A view counts observations, matching the app's calendar-bucket convention; seven observations have six endpoint intervals. Boundaries 300/200 are primary; 100/50 are context. Prespecified next milestones are respectively 200/150/50/25. They are rank milestones, not resistance prices or return forecasts.

At every signal require the coin's numeric CMC ID, positive finite rank and price, and fresh USD quote throughout the viewed observations. Quote age must be between zero and one hour relative to each snapshot decision. Future membership is never used to select a coin. A fresh crossing has previous rank > boundary and signal rank <= boundary, and signal rank must remain > boundary/2. Fractional velocity is log(first rank / last rank)/(view-1), so a 10-rank change does not mean the same thing everywhere. Price momentum is log(last price / first price)/(view-1). Candidate rank-velocity detector additionally requires positive fractional velocity and positive price momentum. This tests price-confirmed rank acceleration without a fitted threshold.

Four ten-slot baskets, with deterministic numeric-ID tie breaks: (1) positive-price/positive-rank fresh crossers ordered by fractional rank velocity, (2) the same candidates ordered by price momentum, (3) all fresh crossers ordered by numeric ID, and (4) positive-price coins inside the same boundary/2-to-boundary rank band ordered by price momentum. The crossing-only baseline is deliberately outcome-independent; it is a descriptive control, not an optimized strategy. Every strategy is evaluated on exactly the same mature signal dates per cell. Fewer than ten selections leave cash. Record occupancy and selection overlap, since sorting makes no difference when candidates do not fill ten slots. The band-momentum comparison changes both crossing gate and occupancy; report both fixed-capital returns and invested-position averages to expose that distinction.

Entry: first saved snapshot after signal. Fixed holds: daily 1/7/14/30 and hourly 1/3/6/12 saved steps after entry, within the same contiguous block. No bridging gaps. Fees 0.5% and 1% each side: gross * (1-fee)/(1+fee)-1. Missing entry stays cash; missing exit is total loss in primary results and zero gross return with fees in sensitivity. These are overlapping equal-weight basket observations, not compounded portfolio wealth or exchange fills.

For each position, evaluate observed rank touching its milestone, rank at exit, crossing back outside the original boundary, and sampled-price touches of +20%, +100%, +400% relative to actual next-snapshot entry. Full future paths begin at entry and end at exit inclusive. Missing path observations make an unobserved touch unknown; a recorded touch remains known. These snapshots cannot establish intraday highs, order fills, or executable stop levels. Report endpoint net returns and observed downside separately from touches.

Save every cell, basket and position, including cash, negatives and unavailable cells. Report unique assets and dates, missing entries/exits, median/mean returns, remove-best-date mean, and paired same-date strategy differences. Split each mode's chronologically ordered mature signal dates in half using dates only, purge early observations whose exits reach the later half's first entry, and report early/later results. This is a chronological stability check on familiar data, not an independent holdout. Report a deterministic nonoverlapping subset starting at each block's first mature signal, spacing H+1 snapshots, without claiming statistical independence across coins.

Data controls: preserve provider ranks and supply, do not reconstruct them from current capitalization; record rank improvements with nonpositive prices, supply changes >5% across the view, missing/censored paths, stale quote exclusions, coverage and raw catalog arrivals/departures. Empty source tags prevent defensible comprehensive stablecoin exclusions; preserve the source population and flag this limitation. Historical Coinbase/Kraken tradability is unavailable. Do not transfer this replay into US-venue performance or calibrated coin-specific probabilities. No claim of easy top-150 arrival or 5x returns follows from rank movement alone.
